Privacy Policy

1. Overview

NOC for FGT is designed as a local-first FortiGate monitoring companion. This policy explains what the app and this website are intended to collect, store, and transmit.

2. Website Data

This static website does not include account registration, forms, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. If hosted on GitHub Pages or another hosting provider, that provider may process basic technical logs such as IP address, user agent, request time, and requested URL according to its own policies.

3. App Data

The app may store device connection details, preferences, trusted certificate fingerprints, and authentication material needed to connect to user-configured FortiGate devices. Sensitive credentials should be stored using platform-provided secure storage such as the iOS Keychain.

4. No Cloud Backend

NOC for FGT does not require a product-operated cloud relay to monitor your FortiGate devices. Device communication is intended to occur directly between the app and the FortiGate address configured by the user.

5. Certificate Trust

If the user enables a trusted certificate option, the app may record certificate fingerprint information locally to help detect certificate changes during future connections. Certificate names or chain properties may be shown for visibility, but the user's explicit trust decision controls whether an exception is allowed.

6. Data Sharing

The product does not sell personal data. Data may be shared only when required to operate user-requested services, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or diagnose issues that the user intentionally reports.

7. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring they have authorization to connect to the FortiGate devices they configure, and for protecting device credentials, network access, and exported diagnostic material.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the product evolves. Material changes should be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy requests, contact support@opshome.run or use the support contact provided with the App Store listing or project repository.
